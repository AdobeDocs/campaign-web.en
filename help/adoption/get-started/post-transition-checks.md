# Checks and troubleshooting after the transition {#post-transition-checks}

After you transition from Campaign Standard to Campaign v8, some deliveries, workflows and landing pages may behave differently from what you expect. The table below lists symptoms reported by transitioned users, with the cause and what to do. For the full list of differences, see [Differences between Campaign Standard and Campaign v8](acs-v8-differences.md).

| Symptom | Cause and what to do | Learn more |
|---|---|---|
| Links in a delivery are direct URLs, and no click data is collected. | Tracking is disabled on the delivery or on the delivery template it is based on. Check that **Activate tracking** is enabled, in particular on the templates of your recurring deliveries. | [Tracking settings](../../v8/advanced-settings/delivery-settings.md#tracking-tab) |
| Recipients who unsubscribed through the "mailto" List-Unsubscribe method are not in quarantine. | This is expected. These recipients are not sent to quarantine. They are unsubscribed from the service associated with the delivery, or added to the denylist if the delivery has no service. To track these unsubscriptions, check the subscriptions of the service or the **No longer contact** section of the profile. | [Quarantines](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/campaign/campaign-v8/send/failures/quarantines){target="_blank"} |
| A landing page does not save a value that is computed by a script or stored in a hidden field. | Landing pages created in the Campaign Web user interface do not support hidden input fields or custom JavaScript. Create the form as a web application in the client console instead. | [Landing pages](../../v8/landing-pages/get-started-lp.md) |
| **File transfer** does not find the file generated by a direct mail activity. | The outbound transition of a direct mail activity carries the targeted population, not the extraction file. Add a **Data extraction** activity, then the **File transfer** activity. | [Direct mail](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/campaign/campaign-v8/send/direct-mail){target="_blank"} |
| Several waves of a delivery are sent together. | Waves are scheduled from the contact date of the delivery. If the delivery starts after the planned time of some waves, those waves are sent together instead of being spaced out. | [Send using waves](../../v8/advanced-settings/send-using-waves.md) |
| A file mask with characters after the asterisk finds no file in Azure Blob Storage. | Azure Blob Storage filters files by name prefix. Only a trailing asterisk is supported. | [Transfer external account](../../v8/administration/transfer-external-account.md) |
| A mirror page of a delivery sent before the transition does not open. | Mirror pages of deliveries sent from Campaign Standard before the transition are not available, and there is no workaround. Mirror pages of deliveries sent from Campaign v8 work as usual. | [Differences between Campaign Standard and Campaign v8](acs-v8-differences.md#admin-tracking) |
| A landing page or content block that uses custom JavaScript behaves differently. | Custom JavaScript is transformed during the migration, but validate and adjust it, in particular if it uses `queryDef`, Campaign JavaScript APIs, session writes, organizational unit handling or custom URL generation. | [Landing pages](../../v8/landing-pages/get-started-lp.md) |
